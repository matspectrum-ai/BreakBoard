# BreakBoard Vision

BreakBoard is a tactical board roguelite in which the player builds an army, transforms the board, and changes the laws governing battles during a run.

The intended payoff is: **“This feels completely broken — and I built it.”**

## Core proposition
1. **Piece Mutations** alter individual pieces or archetypes.
2. **Board Mutations** alter board topology or tile behavior.
3. **Rule Mutations** alter global battle rules.

The game should create emergent combinations rather than rely on thousands of handcrafted scenarios.

## Player experience
- Base rules are learned quickly.
- Battles are short and tactically dense.
- Runs develop distinct strategic identities.
- Mutation choices create agency rather than pure randomness.
- The board visibly tells the history of a battle as it changes.
- Strong combinations feel discovered and constructed by the player.

## Production thesis
BreakBoard is designed to be implementable primarily by coding agents using code, structured data, procedural systems, reusable UI, geometry, icons, effects, and a constrained asset pipeline. High adoption and commercial success are goals, not guarantees.
