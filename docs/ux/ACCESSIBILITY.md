# Accessibility Baseline v0.1

Status: canonical UX requirement.

## Information redundancy
Critical state cannot be encoded by color alone. Owner, legal/capture/danger states, Core classification, Collapse warning, Portal linking/inert status, and board hazards require at least one additional shape/icon/pattern/text channel.

## Vision
- high-contrast support;
- scalable UI text;
- readable exact mechanical text;
- distinct silhouettes/symbols for piece archetypes;
- missing tile visibly distinct from empty tile;
- color-blind-safe state combinations.

## Motion
- reduced-motion mode;
- animation speed control;
- screen shake toggle;
- no mandatory gameplay timing tied to animation;
- rapid effects may be collapsed into concise transitions without losing state explanation.

## Input
- keyboard navigation for menus and battle actions;
- remappable desktop controls;
- no critical hover-only action;
- touch Inspect available via explicit gesture such as long press;
- Cancel/Back behavior consistent across screens.

## Cognition / explainability
- concise plus exact mechanical text;
- deterministic illegal-action reasons;
- persistent access to active rules;
- action/resolution history;
- Boss mechanics disclosed before and during battle;
- irreversible choices explicitly confirmed.

## Audio
Audio may reinforce state but may not be the sole carrier of critical gameplay information. Detailed audio-accessibility design is deferred to the Audio/Presentation gate.
