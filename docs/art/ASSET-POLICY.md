# Asset Policy v0.1

Status: canonical production constraint.

## Priority order
When multiple approaches are viable, prefer:
1. procedural;
2. vector/code-generated;
3. reusable authored/generated asset;
4. AI-generated decorative asset;
5. bespoke handcrafted asset.

This is a cost/consistency preference, not a ban on art craft.

## Procedural-required gameplay presentation
Prefer runtime/procedural definitions for tile surfaces, selection/legal markers, Core halo, owner indicators, Collapse warnings, movement trails, capture feedback, mutation/rule trigger flashes, Portal motion, tile fracture/destruction, and spawn/death fragments.

## Vector/reusable core assets
Use consistent vector/reusable definitions for piece silhouettes, mutation/rule/board glyphs, encounter icons, Boss emblems, and navigation symbols.

## AI-generated assets
AI generation is appropriate for decorative/non-semantic assets such as main-menu backgrounds, optional region environments, optional Boss splash/key art, marketing banners/capsule art, social assets, and cosmetic theme exploration.

AI-generated imagery must not become the sole source of gameplay-critical iconography, piece identity, Board Feature identity, or UI control symbols because reproducible consistency and accessibility take precedence.

## Manual curation
All generated decorative assets require human/agent curation for visual consistency, licensing/provenance requirements applicable at production time, accessibility, composition, and absence of misleading gameplay semantics.

## Cosmetic boundary
Cosmetics may never reduce readability of Core status, owner, mutation count, Board Features, Collapse warnings, legal/capture targets, or other canonical UX state.
